## Negative findings preserved, not suppressed
- SUSHI human preference replay: all five methods issued zero certificates on both panels, 40 seeds each; no suitable first-eligible human prefixes under fixed settings.
- Independent APA empirical human-kernel study: **original AFA 0/40 in APA2000 versus Greedy 26/40 and Joint-Mix 33/40**; all 0/40 in APA2001. The preregistered six-stratum short-horizon confirmation was infeasible.
- V3: no independent full-online certificate-rate improvement against Greedy or validated 5-label human advantage.
- CBA/CFR-3 online reformulations did not beat Greedy/Joint-Mix in their locked synthetic tests.
- IFC is a promising **label-efficiency** result, not proof of independent novelty, human superiority or new optimal sample complexity bounds.
